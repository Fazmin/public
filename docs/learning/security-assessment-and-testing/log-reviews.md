# Log Reviews

## Description

Log reviews involve the continuous monitoring and analysis of logs generated by systems, applications, and network devices to identify abnormal activities or potential security incidents. This practice is crucial for detecting signs of unauthorized access, data breaches, and system anomalies. Regular log reviews help organizations maintain situational awareness and ensure that any security threats are detected and addressed promptly.

## Why This Matters

Log reviews matter because they provide continuous visibility into system, application, and network activity, allowing the organization to detect anomalies, potential security incidents, and unauthorized access quickly. By analyzing logs, staff can identify early warning signs of attacks, misconfigurations, or policy violations, which helps prevent data breaches, service disruptions, and compliance failures. Regular log reviews strengthen the organization's overall security posture and support informed decision-making for incident response. Enterprise operations depend on monitoring IT systems, administrative applications, and network infrastructure. Log reviews help operational teams:

- Detect unusual or suspicious activity that may indicate security threats.
- Ensure that system and network events comply with internal policies and regulatory requirements.
- Provide actionable insights for responding to incidents promptly.
- Support auditing and accountability by maintaining a clear record of events.
- Reduce downtime and service disruptions by identifying issues early.
- Monitor logs to detect misconfigurations, security incidents, and unusual network activity, enabling faster mitigation.
- Track access to shared systems and maintain accountability for sensitive data.

Without regular log reviews, minor anomalies could escalate into larger incidents, affecting administrative workflows and critical services. By continuously reviewing logs, the organization can proactively address security risks, maintain operational stability, and ensure a secure environment.

## Learning Resources

> **Scope and privacy note:** Logs are only useful if they are trustworthy and actually reviewed. Centralize logs off the systems that generate them so an attacker who compromises a host cannot simply erase the evidence, and protect that store as sensitive. Decide in advance what to collect: too little and you miss the incident, too much and real signals drown in noise and storage costs balloon. Logs also capture detailed records of people's activity, so collect and retain only what is necessary, disclose monitoring through policy, follow retention rules, and restrict who can read the logs — review them to protect systems, not to surveil individuals beyond what policy allows.
